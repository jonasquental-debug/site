About

I photograph the world with a banker's eye for detail.

Before picking up a camera seriously, I spent years in investment banking — reading cities through spreadsheets, deals, and late-night office windows. That trained eye for structure, light, and the story beneath the surface now shapes every frame I take.

I work across street, landscape, and urban photography. Based between Paris and the world. Available for editorial, brand, and commercial projects.
